Planned workshops

Our programs

We’re building three workshop programs. They are planned, not running yet, and registration isn’t open. Join the interest list, and we’ll tell you when one opens.

Planned · 60-minute pilot in development

Scam-Savvy Seniors

Learn to spot scams and check before you act, including new tricks that use AI.

You’ll practice

  • Checking a surprise call with the callback habit
  • Spotting fake texts, emails and pop-ups
  • Knowing what AI voice and video fakes can and can’t do
  • What to do and who to call if something goes wrong
Join the interest list for Scam-Savvy Seniors

Planned

Tech Without Tears

Friendly, hands-on help with the phone or tablet you already have.

You’ll practice

  • Saving contacts and favorites
  • Making video calls with family
  • Making text bigger and easier to read
  • Updating your phone safely
Join the interest list for Tech Without Tears

Planned

Aging Smarter at Home

Technology that supports independence, connection and comfort at home.

You’ll explore

  • Phone and tablet accessibility settings
  • Reminders for appointments and medicines
  • Smart speakers and smart-home settings, with privacy in mind
  • Simple ways to stay in touch with friends and family
Join the interest list for Aging Smarter at Home

Workshop format and cost: to be decided (TBD). Dates and locations are not set yet. The interest list is not a registration.
