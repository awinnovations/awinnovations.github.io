Our first guide

A familiar voice is not proof of identity

How to check a surprise call for help

Computer programs can now copy a voice from a short recording. A scammer can call and sound like someone you love. Caller ID can be faked, too.

You don’t need to spot a fake voice. You just need to check a different way.

The callback

  1. Pause. A real emergency can wait two minutes.
  2. Hang up. Say, “I love you. I’ll call you right back.”
  3. Call back on a number you already know. Use your contacts or paper list, not the number that called and not a number the caller gives you.
  4. Can’t reach them? Call someone else you trust. Even if the caller said to keep it secret.
  5. Send nothing until you’re sure.

Good to know: A family code phrase can be a helpful extra check. It does not replace calling back.

Signs to slow down

It’s urgent. It’s about money. It’s a secret. It asks for gift cards, a wire, cryptocurrency, a payment app or a cash pickup.

These steps lower your risk. No step can promise you’ll never be targeted. Sources are listed in the guide, with dates.

Is Mae a real robot I can get?

No. Mae is a made-up character in our comic. She’s not a device or service.

What is voice cloning?

Voice cloning is when a computer program copies how a person sounds. Scammers can use a short recording to make a call that sounds like someone you love. That’s why we teach the callback.

Does a family code phrase keep me safe?

It can help as an extra check. But it doesn’t replace calling back on a number you already know.

I think I was scammed. What should I do?

First, it’s not your fault. If you paid, call your bank or the payment company right away. Then report it at ic3.gov and ReportFraud.ftc.gov. If you’re 60 or older, the National Elder Fraud Hotline can help: 1-833-FRAUD-11 (1-833-372-8311). Our guide has more steps.

Can you help me with my scam case or account?

We teach and share information. We can’t investigate scams, recover money or log in to your accounts. Please contact your bank and the agencies listed in our guide.

Will you send me a printed newsletter?
