Accessibility

We want this site to be easy for everyone to use, including people who use screen readers, keyboards, magnifiers or larger text.

What we’ve done

  • Large, readable text in Atkinson Hyperlegible Next, a typeface designed for low-vision readers.
  • Color combinations checked for contrast. Copper is used for decoration, not for important text.
  • Every control works with a keyboard, with a visible focus outline.
  • Animations are short and gentle. They switch off if your device asks for reduced motion, and Mae’s greeting has a pause button.
  • Ask Mae (demo) opens in a window with large text and buttons. Close it with the Close button or the Escape key, and you return to where you were. New answers are announced to screen readers.
  • The comic is set as real text, with a full transcript. Images have text descriptions.
  • The guide is available as a web page and as a large-type PDF.
  • Forms have clear labels, and error messages are written out in words, not shown by color alone.

Known gaps

  • Finished comic artwork is still in production. The script and transcript are complete.
  • We haven’t yet tested this site with older adult readers. We plan to, and we’ll fix what we learn.

Tell us what’s hard to use

If anything on this site is hard to use, tell us at hello@awinnovations.org and we’ll help you get the information another way.
