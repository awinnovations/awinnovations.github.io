Who we are

About AgeWell Innovations

AgeWell Innovations helps older adults and caregivers understand technology, recognize scams and build confidence through practical education.

What we believe

  • Older adults are capable learners. Nobody is “too old” for technology.
  • Good teaching is calm, clear and kind. No shame. No scare tactics.
  • Safety habits matter more than knowing every new trick.
  • Caregivers need simple, trustworthy tools, too.
Who we serve
Older adults and caregivers across the United States.
Location
United States. Our first events are being planned in Nashville, Tennessee.
Founder
Dominique London is the founder of AgeWell Innovations, an initiative helping older adults and caregivers understand technology, recognize scams, and build confidence. AgeWell combines practical education with approachable storytelling through Mable & Mae.
Where we are now
We’re just getting started. Our programs are in planning. Our first guide is ready to read. Our newsletter and comic are previews, with first issues upcoming. We’ll post real updates here as they happen.
