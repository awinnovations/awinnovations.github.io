Mable & Mae Take on the Modern World

Meet Mable. She’s sharp, funny and always makes her own call. Her small bot friend, Mae, tags along as Mable takes on the modern world one tricky moment at a time. Mable doesn’t trust Mae at first. Over time, curiosity, a little friction and some honest talk turn into trust that Mae has to earn. Mae respects Mable’s choices. Mable stays in charge.

Mae is a made-up character for our stories. She is not a device you can buy. On this website, “Ask Mae” is a demo helper with prewritten answers.

Mable & Mae Take on the Modern World. Episode 1: “The Callback.” Fictional scenario.

Panel 1. Mable, an older woman with silver curls, round glasses, a navy cardigan and a copper scarf, sits at her kitchen table with coffee. Her small bot friend Mae sits next to her mug. Mable is on the phone. A voice on the phone says: “Grandma, it’s Jordan! I’m in trouble. I need $2,000 in gift cards. Please don’t tell Mom!” Mable thinks: “Hmm. Fast, secret and gift cards?”

Panel 2. Mable says into the phone: “I love you, baby. I’ll call you right back.” She hangs up. She opens her contacts and taps the number saved as “Jordan (grandson).” Mae leans in to watch.

Panel 3. The real Jordan answers from his lunch break, holding a sandwich. He says: “Hey, Grandma! I’m just on my lunch break. Everything okay?” Mable smiles. Mae’s eyes look happy.

Panel 4. Mable leans back with her coffee and says: “I’m fine. Your fake twin needs a job.” Jordan laughs and says: “Ha! Love you, Grandma.” Footer: “A familiar voice is not proof of identity. Hang up. Call back on a number you know.”

Ask Mae

Questions about technology, AI, a suspicious message or AgeWell? Mae walks you through short, clear next steps.

Demo: Mae’s answers here are prewritten, not live AI. Nothing you type is saved or sent.

Ask Mae needs JavaScript. You can still ask a person at hello@awinnovations.org. Email is not emergency support.

What Mae can do

  • Explain technology and AI in plain words
  • Help you think through a suspicious call, text or email
  • Suggest next steps that you take yourself

What Mae won’t do

  • Pretend to be a person. Mae is an AI helper character.
  • Send messages, submit forms, make payments or open accounts
  • Tell you a voice, photo or message is real

For anything else, ask a person. Email is not emergency support. If someone is in danger, call 911.
